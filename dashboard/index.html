How the value is computed

Every protocol on this board records no token on DefiLlama and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. Its implied launch value is its own claim, computed from the snapshot's figures alone — no list of names is hardcoded anywhere in the program:

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × age × chains

  • Category multiple — the median FDV/TVL of the launched protocols in the same category (global median when a category has fewer than three launched comps), clamped to 0.02–1.50.
  • Growth — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% and halved into a factor of 0.75–1.50.
  • Age — years listed with no token, +4% a year, capped at +16%. A protocol that survived years at size with no token is a more credible launch than a two-month-old one.
  • Chains — +2% per chain it runs on, capped at +10%.

Implied value is this radar's own claim, never a promise. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. Farm sizes are suggestions, not advice.
